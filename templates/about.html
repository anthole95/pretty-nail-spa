Our Story

Pretty Nail Spa was founded with a simple belief — every person deserves to feel pampered, confident, and cared for. What started as a dream has grown into a welcoming space where clients become family.

Located in the heart of Brighton, CO, we've been serving our community with dedication, creativity, and a whole lot of love. From classic manicures to intricate nail art, every service is performed with attention to detail and a commitment to quality.
